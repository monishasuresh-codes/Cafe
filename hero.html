The Table Story

where every table holds a story


More than a cafe - The Table Story blends delicious food, handcrafted beverages,
a lending library, and a thoughtfully curated bookstore into one inspiring beachside destination.
Enjoy a peaceful atmosphere where you can read, dine, work, or simply unwind with the sound of the waves. Become a library member to borrow books, join our engaging reading sessions and community events, and experience a space created to inspire meaningful conversations, lasting memories, and a lifelong love for reading.
